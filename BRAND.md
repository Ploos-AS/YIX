# µIX brand and compatibility policy (draft)

The MIT and CC BY 4.0 licenses do not grant trademark rights. Accurate descriptive references to µIX and its specification are encouraged, without implying endorsement, affiliation or certification. Independent implementations may describe their self-assessed profile and revision with evidence and limitations.

No official compatibility badge, certification program or trademark registration is asserted. Do not present a self-assessment as independent certification or use project logos to imply approval. Future badge rules and any formal trademark policy require separate review. This draft does not restrict rights otherwise available under applicable law.
