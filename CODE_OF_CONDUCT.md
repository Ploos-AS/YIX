# Code of conduct

Treat participants respectfully, welcome constructive disagreement and focus criticism on technical work. Harassment, threats, discrimination, unwanted sexual attention, publication of private information without consent and retaliation are unacceptable.

Report concerns privately through a verified Ploos AS contact channel. Maintainers should protect confidentiality as far as practical, avoid conflicts of interest and respond proportionately. Possible actions include warnings, removal of content and temporary or permanent exclusion. Appeals may be submitted privately to the steward; where feasible a different reviewer should handle the appeal.

This policy applies to project-managed spaces and representation of the project. A dedicated independent reporting channel remains future work.
