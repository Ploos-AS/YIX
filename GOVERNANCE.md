# µIX open governance

Working proposal. Ploos AS is the initial steward, not a mandatory service provider. Independent operators retain responsibility for their exchanges. This project is not an accredited standards body.

Maintainers appointed by the steward review contributions, publish decisions and manage releases. Technical decisions should seek documented consensus; unresolved disputes are decided by the steward during the initial phase, with rationale and conflicts disclosed. Contributors may appeal through a public issue, except confidential matters. Commercial relationships must not determine technical acceptance.

Normative changes require a µIX Improvement Proposal (MIP), public review (normally at least 14 days), documented compatibility impact and maintainer approval. Security embargoes may justify delayed publication. Editorial fixes may use ordinary pull requests. Releases identify specification revision, toolkit version and known limitations separately. Breaking normative changes require a new specification revision and migration guidance; pre-1.0 compatibility is not guaranteed.

An independent steering group or legal entity may be proposed through a MIP when participation warrants it. No transfer of ownership or governance is implied today. Operator governance and contracts remain separate from framework governance.
