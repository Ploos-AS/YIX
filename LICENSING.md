# Licensing policy

The intended licensing model for µIX is MIT for original project software, configuration, scripts and tests, and Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) for original specification prose, documentation, diagrams and templates.

MIT: https://opensource.org/license/mit
CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode

Third-party materials retain their original terms. Code examples should be explicitly identified as MIT where embedded in documentation. Names, logos and trademarks are not licensed by this policy; a separate brand policy will follow.

Before applying licenses repository-wide, inventory existing contributions and third-party materials, verify rights, add complete license texts and mark applicable files. This policy records the selected model but does not itself grant rights to existing material whose ownership or terms have not been verified.
