## Candidate outreach

Investigate local fibre owners and municipal infrastructure contacts in Sirdal, regional networks such as Altibox/Lyse and national operators such as Telenor, GlobalConnect and Telia. These are research leads only, not confirmed suppliers at the proposed site. Identify actual local network owners and independent ASNs using public coverage information, RIPE records and PeeringDB. Contact existing regional ISPs, hosting networks and public-sector network operators where appropriate.

## Connectivity requirements

Request site-specific quotations for dark fibre, Ethernet transport, IP transit and cross-connects separately. Ask for 1/10GbE options and future 25/100GbE scalability, installation fees, recurring fees, lead times, demarcation, maintenance and fault escalation. Request evidence of physical route diversity, shared ducts, bridges, building entrances, upstream PoPs and common failure domains. Two suppliers do not automatically imply two independent routes.

## Founding participants

Seek at least two independently operated prospective participant networks. Record ASN, contact, desired port, expected traffic, transport requirements and non-binding interest. Apply published eligibility and founding terms equally to affiliated and independent networks. Do not count Ploos alone or multiple Ploos brands as independent participants.
