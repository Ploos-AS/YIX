# µIX governance and operator neutrality — proposal

## Initial structure

Propose Ploos AS as initial legal operator, subject to formal approval and regulatory review. Maintain separate exchange accounting, service terms, operational responsibilities and public identity. Ploos Datacenter may supply facility services under documented terms; Ploos hosting and transit operations are participants on equivalent published terms, not privileged users.

## Neutrality safeguards

Publish admission criteria, port pricing, technical policy and exceptions. Do not require purchase of affiliated services. Allow third-party connectivity subject to objective site and safety requirements. Apply equivalent port provisioning, filtering, incident response and suspension criteria to affiliated and independent networks. Document conflicts of interest, related-party charges and exceptions. Restrict access to participant-specific operational and commercial information.

## Governance

Assign accountable operator, technical lead and incident escalation roles. Invite participant feedback through an advisory forum once independent participants join. Record material policy changes, consultation and reasons for decisions. Provide a complaint and review channel with independent review where feasible. Advisory participation does not confer ownership or voting rights unless separately agreed.

## Evolution options

| Model | Considerations |
|---|---|
| Ploos AS service | Low initial administration; requires credible conflict safeguards |
| Separate subsidiary | Clearer accounting and responsibility; ownership conflicts remain |
| Independent association | Potential participant-led governance; requires statutes, funding and administration |

Review structure annually and when participant demand, conflicts, investment or regulatory requirements materially change. A transfer requires review of contracts, number resources, assets, liabilities and participant consent where applicable.

## Approval gates

Confirm legal operator, Norwegian regulatory obligations, accounting treatment, privacy and contractual terms before launch. Validate practical carrier access and neutrality claims. No independent legal entity, formal governance rights or carrier diversity is established by this proposal.
