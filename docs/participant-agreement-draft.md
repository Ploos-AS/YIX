# µIX participant agreement — discussion draft

Not effective, not an offer of service. Subject to Norwegian legal review and approval before signature.

## 1. Parties and scope
Operator: [confirmed legal entity, organisation number, address]. Participant: [legal entity, organisation number, address, ASN]. The operator provides access to the agreed Layer 2 exchange port; IP transit and hosting are excluded unless separately contracted.

## 2. Service and neutrality
Port capacity, location, demarcation, activation date and optional route-server access are specified in an order form. Bilateral peering is voluntary. Equivalent admission and technical conditions apply to affiliated and independent networks. No purchase of other Ploos services is required.

## 3. Participant obligations
Maintain authorized ASN and prefixes, current technical and emergency contacts, and compliance with the approved peering policy. Prevent disruptive traffic, unauthorized access and misuse of the fabric. Cooperate with incident resolution.

## 4. Operator obligations
Maintain the agreed service with reasonable care, apply published technical policy consistently, communicate planned maintenance and material incidents, and protect confidential operational information. Availability commitments, if any, must be explicitly agreed in a service schedule.

## 5. Fees and term
Fees, VAT, billing interval, activation charges, initial term and notice period are specified in the signed order form. Changes require agreed notice and a defined termination option. No fees are established by this draft.

## 6. Suspension and termination
Proportionate temporary isolation is permitted to protect the fabric during urgent incidents. Record reasons, notify the participant as soon as practicable and provide restoration and review procedures. Non-urgent breaches should normally receive notice and an opportunity to remedy. Define termination, final billing and equipment removal in the final agreement.

## 7. Confidentiality and privacy
Protect credentials and non-public technical and commercial information. Define lawful processing, retention, disclosure and incident notification for contact and operational data. Public participant listings and statistics follow an approved disclosure policy.

## 8. Liability and disputes
Liability limits, exclusions, force majeure, insurance and dispute resolution require legal review. Proposed governing law: Norway. No liability cap or venue is agreed by this draft.

## 9. Annexes and signature
Attach approved peering policy, service order, price schedule, technical specification and any SLA. Define document precedence and change control. Record authorized signatories and effective date.

Before adoption: confirm legal operator, regulatory obligations, privacy terms, commercial terms, liability and operational readiness. Obtain legal review. Do not sign or publish as binding terms until approved.
