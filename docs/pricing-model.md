# µIX pricing model — discussion draft

All amounts are illustrative NOK excluding VAT per month, not market-verified prices or an offer. No service is currently available.

| Product | Proposed monthly fee | Status |
|---|---:|---|
| Founding Participant 1G | 0 for first 12 months | Proposed limited pilot |
| 1G | 250 | Proposed |
| 10G | 750 | Proposed |
| 25G | TBD | Future |
| 100G | TBD | Future |
| Remote peering | TBD | Future |

One port per subscription; optional route-server access and dual-stack peering included. No traffic-volume charge. Cross-connects, transport, optics and installation are separately disclosed where applicable. No mandatory purchase of Ploos services. Founding offer must have published eligibility, duration, capacity and transition terms, equally available to affiliated and independent qualifying networks. No automatic paid conversion without agreed terms.

Participant status is contractual, not legal association membership or ownership. No separate membership fee proposed initially. Governance and voting rights require a separate decision.

## Economics

Illustrative monthly recurring revenue: four paid 1G ports and four paid 10G ports = NOK 4,000 (48,000 annually). This is a scenario, not a demand forecast. Free pilot ports contribute zero revenue. At an illustrative NOK 25,000 equipment investment, 36-month straight-line recovery requires about NOK 695/month before operating expenses. Include allocated facility costs, electricity, resource fees, support, replacement reserve, connectivity and taxes in the full model. Break-even cannot be confirmed until these costs and demand are verified.

## Approval gates

Obtain carrier and facility quotations, validate demand and port capabilities, compare relevant IXP pricing, confirm VAT and legal terms, approve pilot subsidy and publish transparent terms before accepting orders. No prices, purchases or commitments are authorized by this draft.
